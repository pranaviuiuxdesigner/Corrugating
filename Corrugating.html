It all starts with paper.

Every corrugated box begins with high-quality kraft paper. Large paper rolls enter the manufacturing facility to begin their transformation.

Creating the wave.

Heated corrugating rollers transform flat kraft paper into a wave-shaped structure known as fluting.

03

Strength in every layer.

The fluted medium is bonded between flat linerboards to create strong corrugated cardboard. Scroll to watch the layers close.
